Research · Carnegie Mellon Robotics Institute

First-author on two IEEE papers, both under review.

With Prof. Min Xu and Dr. Xiaolong Wu at the CMU Robotics Institute — joint control, safety filtering, and demonstrations on the Franka Emika Panda.

Paper 01
Constraint-Aware Discrete-Time PID Gain Optimization for Robotic Joint Control Under Actuator Saturation
Mishra, O., Wu, X., Xu, M. — arXiv preprint · CMU Robotics Institute. Optimizes discrete-time PID gains for robotic joints under hard actuator saturation, with safety guarantees.
Under review · IEEE TRO
Paper 02
SCIG++: Risk-Limiting Conformal Safety Filtering for Attack-Resilient Robotic Joint Control Under Saturation
Mishra, O., Wu, X., Xu, M. — conformal safety filter for adversary-resilient joint control; algorithms demonstrated on the Franka Emika Panda arm.
Under review · IEEE IROS
